============================================================
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
============================================================

Build the ACADEMIA PORTAL as a real academic-industry intelligence workspace.

Academia should NOT simply show:

- faculty cards
- student lists
- generic statistics
- generic announcements

Academia must answer:

1. What skills is industry demanding?
2. Which skills are emerging?
3. Where are our students weak?
4. Where is the curriculum aligned?
5. Where is practical exposure missing?
6. What faculty opportunities exist?
7. Which industry collaborations can help?
8. Which workshops / projects / mentorships can address the gaps?

Core Academia loop:

INDUSTRY DEMAND
→ EMERGING SKILLS
→ STUDENT SKILL SIGNALS
→ CURRICULUM ALIGNMENT
→ ACADEMIC GAP
→ RECOMMENDED INTERVENTION
→ FACULTY / INDUSTRY COLLABORATION
→ STUDENT PRACTICAL EXPOSURE
→ NEW EVIDENCE
→ UPDATED SKILL INTELLIGENCE
