============================================================
6. DATABASE DESIGN
============================================================

Use proper primary keys.

Use foreign keys.

Use indexes for frequently queried fields.

Use timestamps.

Prefer soft deletion where appropriate.

Use enums/status values for state machines.

Protect against duplicate records.

Use transactions for multi-step updates where needed.

Do not store the same business fact repeatedly.

Examples:

Role skill importance belongs to RoleSkillRequirement.

Student evidence belongs to Evidence.

Skills associated with evidence belong to EvidenceSkill.

A readiness value should be computed from current data,
and snapshots may be stored for historical analytics.

Do not blindly store every derived metric as a separate
editable value.

DERIVED DATA MUST HAVE A CLEAR SOURCE.

12. COMPETENCY VS EVIDENCE CONFIDENCE
============================================================

Keep these separate.

COMPETENCY

Question:

“How strongly does the evidence indicate the student can
demonstrate this skill?”

EVIDENCE CONFIDENCE

Question:

“How trustworthy, recent, diverse and verifiable is the evidence?”

Example:

Machine Learning

Competency:
43

Evidence Confidence:
72%

Do not collapse both into one meaningless score.
