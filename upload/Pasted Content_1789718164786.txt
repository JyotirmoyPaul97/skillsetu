27. TEAM SKILL COVERAGE ENGINE
============================================================

Create:

Problem Statement Skills

Compare against:

Team members' competencies/evidence.

For each required skill calculate coverage.

Use deterministic logic.

Suggested conceptual calculation:

For each required skill:

best team member competency
÷
required competency

Clamp between 0 and 1.

Multiply by skill importance.

Then:

Team Coverage =
weighted covered capability
÷
total required capability
× 100

Make the exact formula visible in the explanation if needed.

Do not hardcode coverage.
