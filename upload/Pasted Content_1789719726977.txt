============================================================
SKILL SETU — PHASE 9
THREE-PORTAL DIFFERENTIATION & INTELLIGENCE UPGRADE
============================================================

IMPORTANT CONTEXT:

The STUDENT PORTAL is already good and should remain visually
and structurally stable.

The current problem is:

INDUSTRY
ACADEMIA
INSTITUTION

feel like generic dashboards.

DO NOT solve this by simply adding more cards, charts or KPIs.

Instead, give each portal a UNIQUE PRODUCT PURPOSE,
UNIQUE WORKFLOW, UNIQUE INFORMATION HIERARCHY and
UNIQUE INTELLIGENCE EXPERIENCE.

============================================================
MASTER PRINCIPLE
============================================================

All four portals belong to one ecosystem.

BUT:

They must NOT look or behave like the same dashboard
with different labels.

Use the following product identities:

STUDENT
=
CAREER & SKILL INTELLIGENCE

INDUSTRY
=
TALENT + DEMAND INTELLIGENCE

ACADEMIA
=
INDUSTRY–CURRICULUM + FACULTY COLLABORATION INTELLIGENCE

INSTITUTION
=
INSTITUTIONAL SKILL INTELLIGENCE + DECISION SUPPORT

============================================================
DO NOT BREAK
============================================================

Do NOT redesign the Student Portal.

Do NOT redesign Phase 1 landing page.

Do NOT create a second design system.

Do NOT create duplicate skill/readiness/opportunity models.

Do NOT replace existing shared data.

Do NOT remove existing features.

Upgrade only:

Industry
Academia
Institution
